# Reading List

## AI

- 2026-02-17 [Machines of Loving Grace](https://www.darioamodei.com/essay/machines-of-loving-grace) by Dario Amodei — "A country of geniuses in a datacenter" — predicts massive leaps in biology, neuroscience, and health from AI, warns autocracy is the real danger, and insists this is all coming within 10 years.
- 2026-02-15 [The Software Factory](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Feb/7/software-factory/) by Simon Willison — Using AI as an independent QA layer with holdout scenarios the agent can't see, separate from the test suite it's free to modify.
